GeoDevelopment Observatory of Cambodia

A public-access platform for analyzing, monitoring, and evaluating sustainable regional development in Cambodia. It brings together environmental, social, and economic indicators from satellite imagery, surveys, and administrative records; makes them explorable through notebooks and web apps; and turns the results into accessible insights.

GeoDevelopment Indicators

  • Human development, income, night-time lights and population (17 provinces, 2013–2019)
  • Population, night-time lights, electricity-based GDP, NPP and PM2.5 (25 provinces, 1990–2023)
  • Estimated GDP per capita (202 districts, 1990–2022)
