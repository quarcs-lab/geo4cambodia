## Overview

An annual panel of Cambodia's 17 Global Data Lab (GDL) subnational regions, 2013–2019. It combines the Global Data Lab's subnational Human Development Index (HDI), gross national income (GNI) and population with harmonized DMSP-like night-time lights (NTL). Some GDL regions group several present-day provinces; for example, KH-GDL-13 is Battambang plus Pailin (see `geo/units.csv`).

- **Dataset id:** `hdi`
- **Categories:** Economy, Health, Social

## Scale: gdl

- **Units:** 17 of 17 gdl units
- **Period:** 2013–2019, annual (119 rows: one per unit and year)
- **Source geometry:** Global Data Lab regions (unions of GADM provinces)

## Notes

- `lnGNIpc` is the natural log of `GNIpc`. The source panel computed it as log(GNIpc × 10^8 / POP), dividing by population twice; the builder recomputes it from `GNIpc`.
- `lnNTLpc` is log(NTL × 10^8 / POP) as in the source panel (POP in thousands): a log of lights per capita shifted by a constant.
- GDL region boundaries are dissolved from the GADM districts of their member provinces, so they nest exactly in the country and contain whole provinces.
