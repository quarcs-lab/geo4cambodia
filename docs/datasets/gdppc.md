## Overview

Annual estimates of GDP per capita (PPP, constant 2017 international dollars) for Cambodia's 202 districts, 1990–2022. They are aggregated from the downscaled gridded GDP-per-capita dataset of Kummu et al. (2025). Alongside the levels, the dataset provides the log of GDP per capita and Hodrick–Prescott trends with three smoothing parameters (λ = 6.25, 100, 400), for studying long-run regional growth and convergence.

- **Dataset id:** `gdppc`
- **Categories:** Economy

## Scale: district

- **Units:** 202 of 202 district units
- **Period:** 1990–2022, annual (6,666 rows: one per unit and year)
- **Source geometry:** GADM 4.1 level 2 (202 districts, as used by Kummu et al. 2025)

## Notes

- GDP per capita cannot be aggregated to provinces without district population weights, so its aggregation rule is `none`. Pass weights explicitly, e.g. `geo.aggregate(df, to="province", rules={"y": "weighted_mean:pop"}, weights=pop)`.
- Original repository: https://github.com/gdo-cambodia/cambodia202-1990-2022-GDPpc
